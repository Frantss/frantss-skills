# Capture

Accept explicit requests and automatically capture clear user-owned commitments
or tasks during current work, without per-item approval.

1. Establish the intended outcome and ownership. A promise by the user supports
   a commitment. Quoted promises, hypothetical examples, and team suggestions
   do not establish the user's ownership. If ownership is uncertain, ask.
2. Search existing records for the same outcome and supporting source before
   creating a task. Add evidence to a match. Keep distinct obligations separate,
   even when they occur in the same message. Resolve ambiguous matches first.
3. Preserve the user's meaning, source reference, and relevant context. Retain
   confirmed deadlines without inferring urgency. Resolve relative dates from
   the statement's date and timezone; ask if material ambiguity remains.
4. Persist under the shared rules. Confirm verified captures with record references;
   batch confirmations at a natural stopping point when several tasks arise.
