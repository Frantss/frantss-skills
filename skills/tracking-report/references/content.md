# Report content

Adapt length to the request. Organize findings by existing workspace and work/personal area, retaining the user's requested scope.

| Include when supported | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| Completed outcomes | Explicit user report or evidence satisfying the outcome, with a supported occurrence date |
| Other contributions and progress | PR reviews, commits, discussions, investigations, decisions, recordings, or other dated activity |
| Committed work and timing | Next/Later tasks, supported promises, firm due dates, and expected completion dates, labeled with the status cutoff |
| Inbox, Maybe, and acknowledgment | Awaiting-triage tasks, tentative possibilities separately from commitments, and tasks missing `reviewed-by-frantss` |
| Work across workspaces | Observed distribution of contributions and pending work, without an invented target ratio |
| Collection coverage | Sources/windows checked, collection times, missing scopes, truncation, and unsaved evidence |

Deduplicate repeated source events by evidence identity. Group related observations under the same outcome when supported.
A PR, deployment, and announcement may describe one outcome; distinct reviews or replies remain distinct supporting activities.
Attribute the user's contribution without claiming ownership of another person's whole deliverable.
A merged subtask can show progress while a broader release remains pending. Closed status alone does not establish a completion date.
An untracked contribution can appear directly; a report does not need a completed task for every observed activity.

Count distinct tasks/outcomes only when their identities are established. Avoid backlog arithmetic when history or coverage is incomplete.
Use explicit time records or the user's report for hours worked. Calendar duration is scheduled time; commit/message volume does not measure effort.
Empty or inaccessible sources do not prove inactivity. Keep missing evidence distinct from verified absence within a completed scan.
Report actual call/item usage if provided and monetary cost only when supplied by the tool; otherwise mark cost unknown.

Daily reports emphasize Next, relevant dates, and blockers. Weekly reports include Later even when undated, surfacing new and older commitments needing attention.
Group unfinished commitments by overdue firm deadline, due/expected today or this week, passed expected date needing reassessment, and upcoming/undated.
Use the report timezone and declared task cutoff; a date-only deadline becomes overdue the following local day, a timestamp after its instant.
Show overlapping due/expected signals on one task without counting it twice; a passed expectation is not an overdue firm commitment.
Keep Maybe separate even when dated; Inbox means `status: inbox` regardless of workspace. Legacy `pending` awaits triage; past Next/Later is unknown.
Upcoming is a date grouping, not a state. Reports surface blocked deadlines and suggested triage without changing tasks or acknowledgment.
