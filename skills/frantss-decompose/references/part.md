# Develop one part, then question it

Select one major part by dependency or frantss's preference. Identify its place in the agreed outline.
Break down that part as needed, using at most three decomposition levels in total:

1. Major parts.
2. Their subparts.
3. Further divisions of those subparts.

The problem title sits outside these levels. Document sections and record categories do not count as decomposition levels.
Represent parent relationships through indentation or explicit parent IDs; IDs themselves do not encode position or depth.
Use fewer levels when they already support the intended next step.

After breaking down the selected part, run its questionnaire using the entrypoint's conversation rules.
Revisit deferred notes recorded under this part.
Complete the part when its approach and connections are clear enough for the intended next step and frantss accepts it.
Resolve questions that could change that approach or its connections. Retain smaller, nonblocking unknowns as explicit follow-ups.
Then select the next major part. Leave other parts pending until their turn.
