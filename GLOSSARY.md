# Personal tracking

Terms for organizing the user's tasks and activity.

## Language

**Project**:
A named context grouping the user's related tasks, activity, and sources. A project can be ongoing or bounded and can span multiple repositories and local folders.
_Avoid_: Workspace for this tracking concept.

**Task**:
A specific outcome owned by the user, with a clear completion condition. A task can be pending or already completed when discovered.

**Activity**:
An action or interaction, such as a review or discussion. Activity can contribute to a task without establishing a separate owned outcome.

**Evidence**:
Source material supporting a task's ownership, progress, completion, or dates, or documenting activity for a report.

**Parent task**:
A task whose outcome includes smaller, independently trackable outcomes.

**Subtask**:
A task contributing a distinct owned outcome to one parent task, with its own completion condition and state.
